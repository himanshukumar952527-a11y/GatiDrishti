Help & Support

❓ How do I check my train's live ETA?
Go to the "Live ETA" tab, type your train number or name, and press Get Live ETA. Try one of the sample trains shown as quick chips.
❓ Is the data shown here real?
This is a Smart India Hackathon 2026 prototype. ETA, schedule and coach data shown are demo/reference values used to illustrate the design — not live official data.
❓ Who do I contact for real railway complaints?
For official assistance, use Rail Madad, call 139 (security/medical), or visit the NTES/IRCTC links in the header.
